Selected Publications

  1. KDD AI4Sci
    APCyc: Property-Informed Design of Cyclic Peptides via Automated Cyclization
    Yifan Zhao*, Lang Qin*, and Jintai Chen†
    In KDD 2026 AI4Sciences Track, May 2026
    Poster
    Abs Bib Code

    APCyc is a target-aware de novo cyclic peptide generation framework that explicitly models cyclization-site and linkage-type information while jointly optimizing drug-relevant physicochemical properties. By combining cyclization-aware representations with Bayesian posterior guidance, APCyc enables controllable multi-property optimization for cyclic peptide design.

  2. arXiv
    RoboStressBench: Benchmarking VLM Robustness to Physical Visual Stress in Embodied Scenes
    Leyi Wu*, Yifan Zhao*, Jinjie Zhang*, and 11 more authors
    arXiv preprint, May 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib Code Website

    RoboStressBench evaluates vision-language model robustness to physical visual stress in embodied scenes. It frames stress from an inverse-graphics perspective across Material, Viewpoint, Lighting, and Geometry factors, enabling controlled analysis of recognition, reasoning, and planning under realistic scene-level stressors.
